## 1. Executive lead

**[FACT]** Indigent defense was rebuilt after the county zeroed Public Defender: line 152 fell from **$318,780** (2022\*) to **$0** (2023–2025\*), while Court Appointed Attorney jumped from **$250,000** (2021–2022\*) to **$692,875** (2023–2024) then **$850,000** (2025\*) — Auditor-site books, lines 152–153. **[FACT]** FY2024 Court Appointed Attorney *actual* was **$1,132,068.06** against a **$692,875** original budget (Audit Budgetary Comparison Schedule — General Fund). FY2023 actual was already **$831,289.68** against the same **$692,875** original. **[FACT]** 2025\* still budgets only **$850,000** — **$282,068** below the FY2024 actual. **[FACT]** At Dec. 31, 2024 the audit assigns **$9,100,619.00** Capital Outlay Accumulations in the General Fund, while every budget book on disk prints that SDCL 7-21-51 appropriation line at **$0**. **[FACT]** Voters authorized **$29,650,000** GO bonds Nov. 5, 2024 for a new jail / law enforcement center (Audit Note 13). **[FACT]** Government Buildings spiked to **$5,270,696** (2023) then **$3,372,111** (2024) before falling to **$875,611** (2025\*) — capital appropriated before the bond vote, then pulled back. **[FACT]** Net position fell **$3,111,407.34** in FY2024 (Statement of Activities). **[INFERENCE]** Highest-dollar risks on this seven-year record: (1) court-appointed under-budgeting against two years of overrun actuals, (2) a $9.1M capital assignment invisible in the budget tables, (3) detention bond debt service (now appearing in the 2027 request — see §8), and (4) Operating Transfers Out rising with no destination breakout.  
**[FACT — 2027 request packet]** Public Defender returns at **$115,148** (p.14) while Court App Atty request falls to **$790,000** against 2025 actual **$1,149,850** (p.15). Jail construction ask **$11,552,100** (Fund 301 p.102) + jail bond debt service **$2,405,000** (Fund 401 p.103). Commissioners Contingency request **$300,000** (p.3). GF Op. Transfers requested **$0** after 2025 actual **$9,758,383** (p.69). Packet grand total requested 2027: **$38,692,576** (Budget 2026 column **$53,729,080**).
