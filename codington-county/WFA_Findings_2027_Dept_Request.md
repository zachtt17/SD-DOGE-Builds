- **[FACT]** Act 153.0 COURT APP. ATTY. (p.15): Exp 2023 **$831,289.68** · 2024 **$1,132,068.06** · 2025 **$1,149,849.75** · Budget 2026 **$890,000** · **Requested 2027 $790,000** (of which line 422.00 Court Appointed Attorneys = **$750,000**; misc 422.90 = **$40,000**).
- **[FACT]** Act 152.0 PUBLIC DEFENDER (p.14): zero actuals 2023–2026 YTD and $0 Budget 2026; **Requested 2027 $115,148** (wages $72,436 + benefits/overhead including health $24,898, rent $1,800, travel $800).
- **[FACT]** Combined PD + CAA request 2027 = **$905,148** vs CAA-only 2025 actual **$1,149,850** — still **$244,702 short** of last year’s CAA spend alone; vs 2025 CAA actual the CAA line alone is **−$359,850**.
- **[INFERENCE]** Highest recurring structural miss on the packet. Restoring a PD office without a credible caseload/rate model that closes the gap to audited overruns is budget theater, not a fix. Ask what 2027 caseload assumption supports $750k contracted counsel *plus* a new $115k in-house PD.

- **[INFERENCE]** The Nov. 2024 $29.65M GO bond is now visible in departmental requests. Transparency win vs prior Auditor books that left Debt Service (800) blank — but Board should publish remaining construction draw schedule vs the $11.55M 2027 ask and confirm P&I is fully loaded (interest request $1.455M vs YTD already $1.07M suggests full-year interest is in range).

### 3. Commissioners Contingency pad — $300,000 with $0 shown as expended in this worksheet
- **[FACT]** Act 112.0 COMMISSIONERS CONTIN (p.3): line 429.00 COMMISSIONERS CONTINGENCY — Exp 2023–2026 YTD all **$0.00** on this report · Budget 2026 **$280,000** · **Requested 2027 $300,000**.
- **[FACT]** Cross-check (prior audit BCS / Nate draft): FY2023 contingency *transfers out* were **$218,658.05**; FY2024 **$125,772.19** — i.e. contingency *is* used mid-year even when this request form shows zeros in the “expended” columns (transfers typically reclass to receiving activities).
- **[INFERENCE]** Classic contingency pad. $300k request with blank expended columns on the citizen-facing request form obscures how often the Board raids it. Demand a 3-year contingency draw log by destination activity.

Combined with contingency, Commissioners control **$421k** of soft discretionary ask ($300k + $121k) before program departments.

### 5. Operating Transfers Out — $9.76M expended 2025, $3.44M budgeted 2026, **$0 requested 2027**

## Method & blockers

1. `pdftotext -layout` → 3,153 lines; 103/103 pages yielded text (no OCR failures; no unreadable pages).
2. Parsed 86 fund×activity totals and 707 account lines; travel/OCE/prof services ranked programmatically.
3. Page cites use the packet’s own `Page: N` header.
4. **Blockers / caveats:**
   - 2026 “Amount Expended” is **year-to-date as of the Aug 11, 2026 run date** printed on headers — not a full year. Do not treat 2026 YTD as annual actual.
   - Contingency and transfers show $0 “expended” on receiving-activity accounting; audit transfer logs are the real use record.
   - No vendor / employee names in packet → related-party self-dealing cannot be proven from this file alone.
   - Activity label “CAPITOL OUTLAY” is the packet’s spelling (p.102).
   - E-911 fund activity name truncated to “E” in header parse (Fund 207 Act 225.0, p.78) — dollars intact.
