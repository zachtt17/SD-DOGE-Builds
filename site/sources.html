State of South Dakota

Primary records

Legislative Research Council
Public · no login
sdlegislature.gov
Bills, enrolled text, amendments, committee and floor votes, legislator rosters, session laws, and codified law. The LRC website loads its own pages from public JSON endpoints under /api/; automated use should confirm the site's terms first. Citation of record for every vote and bill.
Bureau of Finance and Management
Public · PDF
bfm.sd.gov
The Governor's Recommended Budget, the adopted budget, and the budget in brief. Citation of record for every state appropriation figure, by page.
Open SD
Public · no login
open.sd.gov
The state's transparency portal: expenditures by agency and vendor, payroll, contracts, and revenue. Used to drill from a budget category to the lines beneath it.
Secretary of State
Public · no login
sdsos.gov
Election results, candidate filings, and the current officeholder list. Roster verification for every scorecard.
Department of Education
Public · no login
doe.sd.gov
Fall enrollment and average daily membership by district. The per-capita denominator for education lines.
South Dakota Constitution and Codified Laws
Public · no login
sdlegislature.gov/Statutes
The charter test for L4: whether a function is assigned to the state, a county, a municipality, or a school district by the state's own text.

A note on secondary trackers

Third-party legislative trackers are used here for one thing: finding the record faster. They are never cited as the source of a figure, and no automated retrieval from any of them is used or planned until their terms of use have been read in full.

Authorship. Prepared by Zach Thompson, Watertown, South Dakota. The benchmark is classical-liberal and Protestant in derivation and is disclosed as such on every artifact. This is not neutral civic education and does not claim to be.

Fiscal reviews score no individual official. Scorecards score individuals against a published, re-runnable rubric. The two are never merged.

Questions presume no wrongdoing. Categories flag constitutional weight; they do not declare illegality.
