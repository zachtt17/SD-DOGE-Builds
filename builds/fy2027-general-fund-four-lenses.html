2 · How this document sorts

The four lenses

L1

Nominal

Straight year-over-year from the budget document. Published because omitting it looks evasive. Δ = (FY27 − FY26) / FY26

L2

Real

Both years in constant dollars. One index for the whole document, named with its vintage. Δr = (1+Δ)/(1+π) − 1

L3

Per capita

Divided by the served population where it differs from the state's. This draft uses state population for all lines and says so. PC = FY27 / pop

L4

Proper object

Is this an object of this government at this level? Three categories. They flag constitutional weight; they do not declare illegality.

Clearly governmental Gray zone Constitutionally fraught Requires line review

Authorship disclosed: L4 is applied from a classical-liberal and Protestant reading of the founding record, with declared benchmark frames. It is not neutral civic education and does not pretend to be.

5 · Recommended questions

Answerable from records the state already holds

None presumes wrongdoing. Each converts a delta into a question the Bureau of Finance and Management or the Appropriations Committee can answer.

  1. Education · L1/L3 The category is −0.9% while state aid to education is reported −1.7%. Which lines inside Education grew, by how much, and is any of the growth a reclassification rather than new spending?
  2. Health & Human Services · L2/L4 Of the +$45M, how much is the state share of Medicaid, what FMAP assumption is built in, and what is the five-year trend of the state share since expansion took effect?
  3. Corrections · L1 Of the +8.4%, how much is operating growth versus cost tied to the prison capital decision, and where does the capital appropriation sit — since it is not in this operating table?
  4. Federal funds · L4 / subsidiarity Which federal programs carry maintenance-of-effort or matching obligations that bind future general-fund appropriations, and what is the total general-fund dollar amount so bound?
  5. Unclaimed property · L4 $58M is booked as recurring general-fund revenue. What share of remitted property is ultimately reclaimed, and what is the five-year trend of the amount booked?
  6. Public Utilities Commission · funding source A +55.5% figure was reported at the line level. Is that general fund or other funds? Characterizing who pays requires the funding source first.
  7. Finance & Management · L1 A +24.2% figure was reported at the line level. Which line, and is it one-time?
  8. "Other" · L4 One item, +12.0%. What is it?
  9. All categories · trend Provide FY2023–FY2027 general fund by category so that any line characterized as growing or shrinking rests on five years, not one.
  10. Reserves What are the budget reserve and general fund balance positions entering FY2027? They are not on the page that produced these figures.
6 · What this review could not verify

The section that lets the rest stand

  • Every figure on this page was transcribed from a third-party tracker's rendering, not from the BFM budget document. Until re-derived, nothing here is publishable.
  • Whether the FY2026 column is the adopted or revised/estimated FY2026 figure. The two produce different deltas.
  • The deflator, the population level, and the population growth rate are illustrative inputs until replaced with cited BLS and Census figures with vintages.
  • The sales & use tax figure is derived by subtraction, not read from the source.
  • The PUC (+55.5%) and Finance & Management (+24.2%) figures are line-level drill-downs reported from an earlier session and were not re-captured.
  • Per-capita for Education properly uses ADM, and for Corrections both inmate and state population. This draft uses state population throughout.
  • The capital budget and the "other funds" detail are not represented; the all-funds total is used only for the federal share.
  • A five-year trend has not been established for any line. No line on this page is characterized as a trend.
