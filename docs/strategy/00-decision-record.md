# Decision Record

Standing decisions for The Correspondence / SD DOGE Builds. Each carries the condition that
would reverse it. A decision without a reversal condition is a mood.

Format: **D-n — decision** · *Decided* · *Reverses if*

---

**D-1 — Do not rebuild descriptive legislative infrastructure.** Bills, votes, statute diffs,
budget-by-category, campaign finance, federal roll calls are consumed from public sources or,
where terms permit, from the competitor. Never rebuilt.
*Decided:* Sept 2026.
*Reverses if:* a public-source path to a needed input closes and no licensed path exists.

**D-2 — Ship the instrument, not a portal.** Published methodology plus 3–5 fully sourced
scored artifacts. No ingestion, index, or interactive UI in this phase.
*Decided:* Sept 2026.
*Reverses if:* the viability gate PASSES *and* the artifact set has shipped and survived a
hostile reader. Both, not either.

**D-3 — The viability gate blocks all UI and bake-off work.** Ten SD legislators, one axis
(A2), minutes per placement, pre-committed thresholds, blind re-score.
*Decided:* June 2026 (set), Sept 2026 (reaffirmed, thresholds fixed).
*Reverses if:* never. It gets *passed*, not waived.

**D-4 — Fiscal review and benchmark scorecard are separate instruments.** The fiscal review
scores no official. The scorecard discloses authorship on page one. They cite each other and
never merge.
*Decided:* carried from the Watertown DOGE method; reaffirmed Sept 2026.
*Reverses if:* never.

**D-5 — Owned authorship.** The rubric is disclosed as classical-liberal and Protestant in
derivation, every artifact, first page. Never presented as neutral civic education.
*Decided:* carried from the civic-scorecard method.
*Reverses if:* never. The alternative (a neutralized rubric) is a different product.

**D-6 — Benchmark frames carry declared end-dates.** F1 1790s · F2 Story · F3 1860s ·
F4 1880s · F5 open through the Austrians. Rationale published with each.
*Decided:* Correspondence spec v0.3 (June 2026).
*Reverses if:* a frame's rationale fails under review — then the end-date moves *with a
published reason*, never silently.

**D-7 — Public corpora are load-bearing; third-party retrieval is an optional adapter.**
Founders Online, Avalon, LOC, the Anti-Federalist corpus. George AI and any equivalent sit
behind an interface and no citation may depend on them.
*Decided:* spec v0.3.
*Reverses if:* never for the citation rule; adapters may be added or dropped freely.

**D-8 — Two fences.** No analysis fence at any level of government. A structural fence holds
build-out to South Dakota and its own congressional delegation until the SD vertical is proven.
*Decided:* spec v0.3.
*Reverses (the structural fence only) if:* the gate passes, the artifact set ships, and it
survives a hostile reader. The analysis fence has no reversal condition — it is a principle,
not a schedule.

**D-9 — First published artifacts are local, not legislative.** County, council, school board —
the rows of the coverage map that are unoccupied *and* protected by data shape. PUC gets one
early normative artifact because its row will not stay empty.
*Decided:* Sept 2026, on the coverage map.
*Reverses if:* a competitor demonstrates local coverage at scale — then the protection
assumption was wrong and the lane is re-evaluated, not abandoned.

**D-10 — No design assumes automated retrieval from a third-party site until its terms are
read and recorded.** Verification item 7.3.
*Decided:* Sept 2026.
*Reverses if:* the terms, read in full, permit it — recorded verbatim in this repo.

**D-11 — Unverified claims about the competitor are labeled or omitted.** Verified / Reported /
Indicated, every time, and nothing from the lower two tiers is repeated publicly.
*Decided:* Sept 2026.
*Reverses if:* never.

## Trip-wires (checked quarterly, first check December 2026)

- A third-party normative scorecard appears on the competitor's `/integrations` or legislator pages
- Local-government or PUC coverage appears on the competitor's site
- The competitor publishes its per-bill stance/relevance table
